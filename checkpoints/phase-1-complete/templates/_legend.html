How to read the table and cards

Quarter
Three months; the first ten rows are inherited.
Policy rate %
The interest rate set by the central bank; its effect arrives in the next row.
📊 Inflation %
The weighted price rise: 70% core, 20% food, 4% fuel and 6% energy; target 2%.
🧺 Core inflation %
Price rises outside food, fuel and energy; shown in slate.
👷 Unemployment %
People wanting work without a job; normal here is 4.50%; blue on the scoreboard.
Output gap %
How far the economy runs above (+) or below (−) its normal capacity. Above it heats prices; below it costs jobs.
🍞 ⛽ 💡 Cards
Food in amber, fuel in red, energy (electricity and gas) in yellow; arrows compare with last quarter.
💥 Financial stress
💥 beside a quarter means a sharp earlier policy-rate move has hit activity and jobs in that quarter. A move larger than 0.50 points in either direction causes an extra fall in activity and higher unemployment one quarter later. The badge measures the extra output-gap penalty; the icon stays in the record.
✈️ / 🧳 People moving
Green arrivals or red departures this quarter; 👥 population includes them.
